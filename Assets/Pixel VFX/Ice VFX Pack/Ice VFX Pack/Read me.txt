📁 CONTENTS
-------------------------------------------

This VFX pack includes the following:

✅ 64x64 (The Ice rocks - 32x32) pixel Ice-themed visual effects
✅ Provided in two formats:
   - Sprite Sheets (PNG)
   - Individual Frame Sequences (PNG)
✅ Looping and non-looping animations

List of effects:
- Ice v1 [15 frame]
- Ice v2 [3x14 frame]
- Ice v3 [(Coming: 7 frame),(Idle: 12 frame),(Hit: 13 frame (hit fade in: 9 frame),(Stand: 1 frame),(Hit fade out: 3 frame)),(Miss: 5 frame)]
- Ice v4 [23 frame]
- Ice v5 [14 frame]
- Ice v6 [(Rise: 11 frame),(Stande: 1 frame),(Hit: 4 frame),(Destroy: 7 frame)]
- Ice v7 [(Fade In: 7 frame),(Idle: 12 frame),(Fade Out: 8 frame)]
- Ice v8 [(On roof: 1 frame),(Fall: 11 farme),(Destroy: 5 frame)]
- Ice v9 [11 frame]

📌 HOW TO USE
-------------------------------------------

You can import the sprite sheets into your engine or editor and use slicing tools to break them into frames.  
Or use the PNG sequences if your engine supports frame-by-frame animations (e.g. Unity, Godot, GameMaker).

All animations are designed at 64x64 resolution. You can upscale them as needed.
